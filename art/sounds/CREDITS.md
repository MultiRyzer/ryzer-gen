# Sound credits

Every recording below is from Pixabay, under the Pixabay Content License. Credit them on the mod page. `art/tools/machine_sounds.py` cuts them into the loops the mod ships.

The original recordings are not in this repository: the licence allows using them inside the mod, not redistributing them as they are. To rebuild the sounds, download each one from Pixabay into `art/sounds/source/` (ignored by git).

The reactor announcer's voice lines (`sounds/voice/`) are spoken by Ryzer and processed by `art/tools/voice_lines.py`. No AI voice model is involved, so there is no model licence to credit.

The microreactor's running hum is vanilla's beacon hum, pitched down, and the dosimeter click is vanilla's note block hi-hat, pitched up. Neither file is shipped.
