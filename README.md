# Ryzer Gen

Mod ID: `ryzergen`

A NeoForge mod for Minecraft 1.21.1 (the version ATM10 runs on) about power progression through physics: from a small portable microreactor, through a real fuel cycle and fission, up to a Dyson swarm around a sun you can walk on.

**Status: public alpha (0.1.1-alpha).** Tiers 1 to 3 are playable: the microreactor, the battery rack, pipes and steam, the fuel cycle and the fission power station. See `docs/ROADMAP.md` for what comes next.

MIT licensed (see `LICENSE`). Sounds keep their own licence; see `art/sounds/CREDITS.md`.
